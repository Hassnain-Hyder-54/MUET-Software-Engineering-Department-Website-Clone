The program

Computing foundations, shaped for an intelligent future.

MUET lists BS Artificial Intelligence among its undergraduate programs. The program is presented through the Department of Software Engineering at the Jamshoro campus.

Its published curriculum introduces students to intelligent agents, problem-solving and search, knowledge representation, machine learning, natural language processing, expert systems, and the development of programs for real-world problems.
